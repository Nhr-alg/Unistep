    Specimen 01 Physics · Rotation

    A solid sphere rolls without slipping. Find the ratio of rotational KE to total KE.

    Result
    2/7 ratio
    1. STEP 01

      Pure rolling links linear and angular velocity.

      v = ωR
    2. STEP 02

      Use the moment of inertia for a solid sphere.

      I = (2/5)MR²
    3. STEP 03

      Rotational and translational energy combine.

      K = (1/2)Mv² + (1/5)Mv²

    Why it is different

    Built on checks, not confident guesses.

    A calculator gives you an answer. A model can invent one. UniStep shows the governing equation, the derivation, and the dimensional check behind every result.

    ∑

    Symbolic verification

    Results are checked algebraically and, where possible, solved a second way. If the two paths disagree, you see a warning instead of a wrong answer.

    ⇄

    Dimensional analysis

    Units are tracked through every step — nm to m, g to kg, degrees to radians — and the final unit is validated against the quantity you asked for.

    ✎

    Exam-style derivations

    Each solution names the principle, states the governing equation, substitutes with units, and ends with a sanity check. It reads like the solution you are expected to write.

    Questions

    Before you join

    Is this a homework-cheating tool?

    No. It is built for learning and verification: every result comes with the governing equation and a derivation you can follow, check, and reproduce in an exam. We do not write essays, and we do not hide the steps.

    How do you know the answers are correct?

    Every result is checked symbolically, and where possible solved through a second independent path. Units are tracked through each step. If a problem falls outside the verified library, the tool says so instead of guessing.

    What topics will the beta cover?

    The first release focuses on first-year mechanics: statics, dynamics, rotation, energy, momentum, and the unit conversions that go with them. Thermodynamics, circuits, and chemistry follow based on beta demand.

    How does pricing work?

    Students get 10 free verified solves to try the solver. When those are used, the prototype locks on that browser. Paid access is not on sale yet: every paid tier is a waitlist. When billing opens, founding members keep 50% off for 12 months and every paid plan has a 14-day refund window. Tutoring centers can request a two-week pilot through the same list.

    What happens to my data?

    Your problems stay on your device in the app. For the beta waitlist we store your email, role, plan interest, and optional topic answer. You can ask us to delete them at any time; see the privacy policy.
