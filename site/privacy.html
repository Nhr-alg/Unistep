Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

UniStep is an early beta. This policy explains what the waitlist collects, why we collect it, and how to have it removed. It covers this website, not the calculator prototype, which stores your problems in your own browser.

What we collect

  • Waitlist details: your email address, your role (student, tutor, educator, exam candidate), the plan you selected, an optional answer to “hardest topic”, and the time you consented.
  • Campaign context: the page you landed on, the referring URL, and UTM campaign parameters, so we know which channel brought you in.
  • A random visitor ID: generated in your browser and stored in localStorage so repeat visits are not double-counted. It is not linked to your name and is not a cookie.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising or third-party analytics scripts.
  • No tracking cookies.
  • No IP addresses stored in our database. Our hosting provider may keep short-lived standard access logs for security and reliability.
  • No payment details on this site. When paid plans open, payments will be handled by a payment processor; card data never reaches our servers.

Why we use it

  • To invite you to the beta and send launch updates you asked for.
  • To decide which track and topics to build first. We look at aggregate counts, not individual behaviour.
  • To prevent abuse of the signup form (rate limiting).

Who can see it

Access is limited to the person building UniStep. If we use an email or hosting provider, your data is processed on our behalf under their terms. We do not sell personal data.

How long we keep it

Waitlist data is kept until the beta opens, or for up to 12 months if the launch is delayed. Ask us to delete it sooner and we will remove it within 30 days.

Your choices

  • Ask for a copy of what we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct or delete it.
  • Unsubscribe from updates at any time.

Contact us through the beta list for any of these requests.

Children

The beta is intended for people aged 16 and over. If you believe a younger person signed up, contact us and we will remove the record.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date above and, where we have your email, tell you directly.
