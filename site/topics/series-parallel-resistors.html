Series and parallel resistors

In series, the same current passes through every resistor, so voltage drops add and resistances sum. In parallel, every resistor sees the same voltage, so conductances add and the equivalent resistance is smaller than the smallest branch.

Two resistors 4 ohm and 6 ohm in parallel

Answer 2.4 Ω

  1. Identify the Configuration The resistors (4 Ω and 6 Ω) are connected in parallel. 1/Req = 1/R1 + 1/R2 + …
  2. Substitute the Values Add the reciprocals of each resistance, then invert the total. 1/4 + 1/6
  3. Final Answer The equivalent resistance replaces the whole network with a single component. Req = 2.4 Ω

Assumptions: Ideal resistors; Negligible connecting-wire resistance

Three resistors 4 ohm in series

Answer 12 Ω

  1. Identify the Configuration The resistors (4 Ω and 4 Ω and 4 Ω) are connected in series. Req = R1 + R2 + …
  2. Substitute the Values Add the resistance values directly. 4 + 4 + 4
  3. Final Answer The equivalent resistance replaces the whole network with a single component. Req = 12 Ω

Assumptions: Ideal resistors; Negligible connecting-wire resistance
