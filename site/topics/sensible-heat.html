Sensible heat

Sensible heat is the energy needed to change a substance's temperature without changing its phase. It depends on mass, the material's specific heat capacity, and the temperature rise.

Find the heat required to raise 2 kg of water by 10 K

Answer 83.72 kJ

  1. Identify the Principle The heat required is proportional to mass, specific heat capacity and temperature change. q = m · c · ΔT
  2. Look Up the Specific Heat For water, c = 4.186 kJ/(kg·K). c = 4.186 kJ/(kg·K)
  3. Substitute Mass in kg and temperature change in kelvin (a change of 1 K equals a change of 1 °C). q = (2)(4.186)(10)
  4. Final Answer The result is energy, so it is reported in kilojoules. q = 83.72 kJ

Assumptions: No phase change; Constant specific heat over the temperature range; Closed system at constant pressure
