Reynolds number

The Reynolds number compares inertial forces to viscous forces. Below about 2300 viscous effects dominate and flow is laminar; above about 4000 inertia dominates and flow is turbulent.

Find the Reynolds number for water at 2 m/s in a pipe of diameter 0.05 m

Answer 99800

  1. Identify the Principle The Reynolds number is the ratio of inertial to viscous forces in a flow. Re = ρ·v·D / μ
  2. Convert to Base Units Diameter in metres, velocity in m/s, density in kg/m³, viscosity in Pa·s. D = 0.05 m, v = 2 m/s, ρ = 1000 kg/m³, μ = 0.001002 Pa·s
  3. Compute Substitute the properties into the definition. Re = (1000)(2)(0.05) / 0.001002
  4. Classify the Flow Re = 99800 falls in the turbulent regime. Re = 99800 → turbulent

Assumptions: Fully developed flow; Newtonian fluid; Constant properties; Water at about 20 °C
