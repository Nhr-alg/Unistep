Ideal gas law

The ideal gas law combines the pressure, volume, amount and absolute temperature of a gas into one relation. Temperature must be in kelvin, because the law is defined from absolute zero.

Calculate the pressure of 2 mol of an ideal gas at 300 K in 0.05 m3

Answer 99768 Pa

  1. Identifying the Principle We recognize this as a Ideal Gas problem. The most efficient approach is to start from PV = nRT and isolate the unknown. PV = nRT
  2. Substitution Plugging the provided values 2, 300, 0.05, 3 into our expression. This ensures we are working with the correct units and magnitudes.
  3. Final Calculation Evaluating the algebraic expression carefully leads us to the final result.

Assumptions: Ideal gas behaviour; Temperature in kelvin; R = 8.314 J/(mol·K)
