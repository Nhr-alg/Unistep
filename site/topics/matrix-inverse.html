Matrix inverse

The inverse of a 2×2 matrix swaps the diagonal entries, negates the off-diagonal entries, and divides by the determinant. It exists only when the determinant is non-zero.

Find the inverse of [[4,7],[2,6]]

Answer [ 0.6, -0.7 ], [ -0.2, 0.4 ]

  1. Read the Matrix A 2×2 matrix has four entries, read row by row. A = [[4, 7], [2, 6]]
  2. Compute the Determinant Subtract the product of the anti-diagonal from the product of the main diagonal. det(A) = (4)(6) − (7)(2) = 10
  3. Apply the Inverse Formula Swap a and d, negate b and c, then divide every entry by the determinant. A⁻¹ = (1/det) · [[d, −b], [−c, a]]
  4. Final Answer Substituting the entries gives the inverse matrix. A⁻¹ = [ 0.6, -0.7 ], [ -0.2, 0.4 ]

Assumptions: Square 2×2 matrix; Determinant is non-zero
