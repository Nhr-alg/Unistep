Worked examples, verified by the engine

Each page below shows complete working — the governing relation, every substitution, the result with its unit, and the assumptions that make it valid. The answers are generated by the same solver that runs in the app, so a page cannot promise a problem the product cannot solve.

  • Quadratic equations Solve quadratic equations with the discriminant and the quadratic formula, including complex roots, with every step shown.
  • Linear equations Rearrange a linear equation by moving the constant and dividing by the coefficient, then check the answer by substitution.
  • Systems of two equations Solve two linear equations in two unknowns with determinants, and see why a zero determinant means no unique solution.
  • Derivatives of polynomials Differentiate each term of a polynomial with the power rule, including why constant terms disappear.
  • Indefinite integrals Integrate polynomials term by term, raise the exponent, divide by the new exponent, and add the constant of integration.
  • Definite integrals Find the antiderivative, then subtract its value at the lower limit from its value at the upper limit.
  • Determinants Compute a determinant by expanding along the first row, and read what a zero determinant means geometrically.
  • Matrix inverse Swap the diagonal, negate the off-diagonal, and divide by the determinant — with the singularity check shown.
  • Unit conversion Convert between length, time, mass, speed, force, pressure, energy, power and volume units by multiplying by a ratio equal to one.
  • Temperature conversion Convert between Celsius, Kelvin and Fahrenheit by going through the absolute scale, so the offset is applied exactly once.
  • Ohm's law Apply V = IR in all three rearrangements, with the known quantities identified before substitution.
  • Series and parallel resistors Add resistances in series, add conductances in parallel, and check that a parallel result is smaller than the smallest branch.
  • Axial stress Convert force and area to base units, divide, and report the result in MPa or kPa.
  • Strain and Young's modulus Compute strain as change in length over original length, then take stress divided by strain to get the elastic modulus.
  • Reynolds number Compute Re from density, velocity, diameter and viscosity, then classify the flow as laminar, transitional or turbulent.
  • Bernoulli's equation Use the horizontal form of Bernoulli’s equation to find the pressure change when a fluid accelerates or slows.
  • Sensible heat Find the energy needed to change a material’s temperature, using its specific heat capacity and no phase change.
  • Projectile motion Resolve the launch velocity into components, then use constant-acceleration kinematics to find range, height or time of flight.
  • Rolling energy ratios Find the ratio of rotational to total kinetic energy for a rolling sphere, cylinder or ring from its moment of inertia.
  • Ideal gas law Apply PV = nRT to find the pressure of a gas from its amount, temperature and volume.
