Axial stress

Normal stress is the internal force divided by the cross-sectional area it acts on. The same force over a smaller area produces a larger stress, which is why thin sections fail first.

A force of 20000 N acts on an area of 0.00007854 m2. Find the stress.

Answer 254.647 MPa

  1. Identify the Principle Normal stress is force per unit area. σ = F / A
  2. Convert to Base Units Force in newtons and area in square metres give stress in pascals. F = 20000 N, A = 0.00007854 m²
  3. Compute the Stress Divide the force by the area. σ = 20000 / 0.00007854 = 254650000 Pa
  4. Final Answer 1 MPa = 10⁶ Pa, which is the usual unit for engineering stress. σ = 254.647 MPa

Assumptions: Uniform stress distribution; Centric axial load; Material remains elastic
