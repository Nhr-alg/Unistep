Unit conversion

Converting units multiplies by a ratio of conversion factors. Because the ratio equals one, the physical quantity is unchanged — only the number and its label are rewritten.

Convert 90 km/h to m/s

Answer 25 m/s

  1. Write the Conversion Ratio One m/s equals 3.6 km/h, so the ratio is built to cancel the original unit. 1 m/s = 3.6 km/h
  2. Multiply by the Ratio Multiplying by a ratio equal to one changes the units without changing the value. 90 km/h × (0.27777778 m/s/km/h)
  3. Final Answer The original unit cancels, leaving the target unit. 90 km/h = 25 m/s

Assumptions: Exact conversion factors; Same physical quantity on both sides

Convert 5 km to m

Answer 5000 m

  1. Write the Conversion Ratio One m equals 0.001 km, so the ratio is built to cancel the original unit. 1 m = 0.001 km
  2. Multiply by the Ratio Multiplying by a ratio equal to one changes the units without changing the value. 5 km × (1000 m/km)
  3. Final Answer The original unit cancels, leaving the target unit. 5 km = 5000 m

Assumptions: Exact conversion factors; Same physical quantity on both sides
