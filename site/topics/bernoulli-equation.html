Bernoulli's equation

Along a streamline, pressure, kinetic energy per unit volume and gravitational potential energy per unit volume trade off against each other. Where the fluid speeds up, the pressure must fall.

Using Bernoulli, water flows at v1 = 1 m/s and v2 = 3 m/s. Find the pressure drop.

Answer 4 kPa

  1. Write Bernoulli's Equation For a horizontal streamline the elevation terms cancel, leaving pressure and kinetic energy to balance. P₁ + ½ρv₁² = P₂ + ½ρv₂²
  2. Rearrange for Pressure Change Group the velocity terms to isolate the pressure difference. ΔP = ½ρ(v₁² − v₂²)
  3. Substitute Density is taken as 1000 kg/m³ for water. ΔP = ½(1000)(1² − 3²)
  4. Final Answer The pressure rises as the fluid slows, converting kinetic energy back into pressure. ΔP = 4 kPa

Assumptions: Steady, incompressible, inviscid flow; Horizontal streamline; No losses between the two points

Using Bernoulli, water flows at v1 = 4 m/s and v2 = 1 m/s. Find the pressure change.

Answer 7.5 kPa

  1. Write Bernoulli's Equation For a horizontal streamline the elevation terms cancel, leaving pressure and kinetic energy to balance. P₁ + ½ρv₁² = P₂ + ½ρv₂²
  2. Rearrange for Pressure Change Group the velocity terms to isolate the pressure difference. ΔP = ½ρ(v₁² − v₂²)
  3. Substitute Density is taken as 1000 kg/m³ for water. ΔP = ½(1000)(4² − 1²)
  4. Final Answer The pressure falls as the fluid accelerates, which is the basis of a Venturi meter. ΔP = 7.5 kPa

Assumptions: Steady, incompressible, inviscid flow; Horizontal streamline; No losses between the two points
