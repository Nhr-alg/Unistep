Definite integrals

A definite integral accumulates a function between two limits. Evaluating the antiderivative at the upper limit and subtracting its value at the lower limit gives the exact signed area.

Evaluate the integral of 2x + 3 from 0 to 2

Answer 10

  1. Identify the Integrand Write the expression in standard polynomial form. f(x) = 2x + 3
  2. Apply the Power Rule for Integration For every term axⁿ, the antiderivative is a·xⁿ⁺¹/(n+1). ∫ a·xⁿ dx = a·xⁿ⁺¹/(n+1)
  3. Find the Antiderivative Integrate each term independently. F(x) = x^2 + 3x
  4. Evaluate at the Limits Apply F(2) − F(0). F(2) − F(0) = 10
  5. Final Answer The definite integral is the accumulated area under the curve. ∫ from 0 to 2 = 10

Assumptions: Polynomial integrand; Integration with respect to x
