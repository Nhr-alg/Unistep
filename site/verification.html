What is covered

  • Mechanics: rolling-energy ratios, uniform acceleration, motion on a rough incline, projectile range, height and flight time.
  • Materials: axial stress, strain, Young's modulus from load, area and elongation.
  • Fluids: Reynolds number with regime classification, Bernoulli pressure change.
  • Thermodynamics: sensible heat for water, aluminium, copper, steel and air; ideal gas law.
  • Circuits: Ohm's law in all three rearrangements, series and parallel equivalent resistance.
  • Mathematics: quadratic roots (including complex), linear equations, 2×2 systems, derivatives and integrals of polynomials, definite integrals, 2×2 and 3×3 determinants, 2×2 inverse.
  • Units: length, time, mass, speed, force, pressure, energy, power and volume, plus Celsius, Kelvin and Fahrenheit.

What it will not do

  • It will not solve a problem outside those families. It returns “outside the verified library” rather than an invented answer.
  • It will not read an arbitrary photo of a diagram. OCR extracts text; it does not interpret a free-body diagram.
  • It will not claim a symbolic proof. Derivatives and integrals are polynomial-only, by design.
  • It is not a substitute for your course's conventions on rounding, sign or significant figures.

Why the test set is public

A calculator that hides its test set is asking you to trust a marketing claim. Ours is a file you can read, a button you can press, and a result you can reproduce. When a module regresses, the suite fails before a user sees it.
